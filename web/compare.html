Where Krovi is still behind today

Honesty is the brand, so here is the candid part. Krovi is new, and a mature paid app has advantages we don’t pretend away.

Last reviewed 1 November 2026. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue and we’ll fix it.

  • Windows only. There is no macOS, Linux or mobile build. If you need cross-platform today, Krovi isn’t it.
  • New and less battle-tested. Established apps have years of real-world use, bug reports and edge-case hardening that we haven’t accumulated. The voice and live-data features of 0.2 have had the least exposure.
  • On-device voice has trade-offs. Recognition that never leaves your PC can trail large cloud services in accuracy and language coverage, and Krovi’s voice is a closed list of commands, not open-ended chat, in English (US) only. That is the price of privacy.
  • Windows 11 is the tested platform. Krovi has only been tested on Windows 11; Windows 10 is untested.
  • Holidays cover three regions. The bundled holiday data covers India, the US and Great Britain for 2025 to 2027 (new in v0.2); other countries are not included yet.
  • Unsigned installer. Krovi isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Checksums are published for every release.
  • “Not verified” is honest, not dismissive. It means we couldn’t confirm a competitor’s feature from public information. It may well exist. We’d rather say “we don’t know” than guess.
