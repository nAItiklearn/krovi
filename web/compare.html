Where Krovi is still behind today

Honesty is the brand, so here's the candid part. Krovi is new, and a mature paid app has advantages we don't pretend away:

  • Windows only. There is no macOS, Linux or mobile build yet. If you need cross-platform today, Krovi isn't it.
  • Brand-new and less battle-tested. Krovi is in early development. Established apps have had years of real-world use, bug reports and edge-case hardening that we simply haven't accumulated.
  • Fewer polish-years. Animations, onboarding, localisation and the hundred small refinements that come from a long-lived product are still ahead of us.
  • No mobile companion. Some suites pair a desktop app with a phone app; Krovi is desktop-only by design for now.
  • "Not verified" is honest, not dismissive. Where a competitor's cell says "Not verified," that means we couldn't confirm it from public information — it may well be present. We'd rather say "we don't know" than guess.
