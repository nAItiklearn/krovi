Where Krovi is behind today

Last reviewed 1 November 2026. Spotted an error? Open an issue on GitHub.

  • Windows only. There is no macOS, Linux or mobile build.
  • Younger codebase. Established utilities have years of edge-case testing that a v0.3 project has not yet accumulated.
  • On-device voice trade-offs. Local speech recognition trails large cloud services in language coverage, and built-in voice commands cover English (US) phrases only.
  • Tested on Windows 11. Windows 10 is supported by target architecture, though untested.
  • Three bundled holiday regions. Offline public holidays cover India, the US and Great Britain for 2025 to 2027.
  • Unsigned installer. Checksums are published on every release while code-signing is still pending.
