VoiceNew in v0.3

Dictate into any app.

Hold a shortcut, speak, and Krovi types your words where your cursor is. Speech is recognised on your PC with Whisper, so there is no cloud speech service and no audio upload.

  • Hold to talk, or toggle. Ctrl + Alt + D by default, and you can change it.
  • You pick the model. Tiny, Base or Small, plus a Base English-only one. It downloads once, when you ask, and its checksum is verified.
  • Audio is never stored. Needs a CPU with AVX2 (2013 or newer).
What is new in v0.3
Agent ModeNew in v0.3

Say what you want. Approve what matters.

Type a sentence such as “open youtube and search youtube for lofi”. Krovi turns it into a short plan on your PC. Safe steps run at once; anything that schedules something, opens an address it does not know or prepares an email waits on a card for your OK.

  • Understood on your PC. A built-in planner, not an AI service.
  • Krovi never sends email. It opens a draft; you press Send.
  • Honest about limits. A step it cannot do is shown as “Not run”, with the reason.
  • Talk to Agent. An optional hold-to-talk shortcut (Ctrl + Alt + A, off until you turn it on). Local Whisper turns your words into text for Agent Mode, and any step that needs approval still shows its card.
Circle to AskNew in v0.3

Circle anything. Ask about it.

Press the shortcut, drag a circle around something on your screen, and Krovi reads just that area, once, on your PC. Copy the text, search it, or ask your own AI provider about it. The picture is never saved.

Ctrl+Alt+C starts it from any app.
  • Only what you circle. Nothing is watched in the background, ever.
  • Screen awareness is separate. You start it, it lasts 5 to 30 minutes, and an indicator stays visible the whole time.
Clean My OSNew in v0.3

Find the junk. Keep the control.

Scan for temporary files, caches, crash dumps and old installers. Krovi lists every folder and file with its real path and size, and moves nothing until you confirm. Everything goes to the Recycle Bin, so you can undo it.

  • You tick what goes. Each group opens to show exactly what is in it.
  • Gentle by design. Files changed in the last 24 hours are left alone, shortcuts are not followed, and Krovi never asks for administrator rights.
Clipboard, Timers, PomodoroFrom v0.2

The small things you open ten times a day.

A searchable clipboard history that updates the moment you copy, several timers at once, and a Pomodoro that keeps counting while the panel is closed and shows in the pill.

  • Clipboard. Text, links and images. Pin what you reuse. Copies from password managers are never stored.
  • Timers and stopwatch. Run as many as you like side by side.
  • Pomodoro. Focus and break cycles, with a count of today’s sessions.
Themes and moodsFour new in v0.3

Make it yours. Let it have moods.

Every colour in Krovi comes from a theme, so switching is instant. When you stop, the pill rests as a pair of eyes that glance at your cursor, blink and wake the moment you touch it.

  • Ten themes. v0.2 has six. v0.3 adds Obsidian, Linen, Voxel and Pixel Meadow.
  • Quick reactions (v0.3): the eyes can look curious, focused, pleased or worried as things happen.

Quick TransferFrom v0.2

From your PC to your phone.

Drop a file and a QR code appears. Scan it with your phone’s camera and the file downloads over your own Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account, no cable.

  • Local network only. A small server runs only while a share is open, then closes itself.
  • Optional PIN and a link that expires on its own.
  • Plain HTTP on your own network, so use a network you trust.

    Windows
    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Tested on Windows 11; Windows 10 is untested.
    Processor
    A 64-bit CPU. Local dictation (v0.3) needs AVX2, found in CPUs from 2013 onward.
    Runtime
    Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is included with Windows 11.
    Network
    Not required. Weather and exchange rates are optional lookups.
    Installer
    Not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm. Checksums and steps.
