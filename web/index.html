How Krovi works from one shortcut

Click the pill or press Ctrl + Alt + Space to reopen the tool you used last, with the other eighteen on the left rail and live memory, disk and CPU rings on Home.

  • Voice dictationCtrl + Alt + D

    Hold or toggle the shortcut to type spoken words at your cursor in any Windows app via local Whisper (Tiny, Base or Small on AVX2 CPUs). Audio is never uploaded.

  • Agent ModeCtrl + Alt + A optional

    Turns a typed or spoken sentence into a local step plan. Safe steps run right away; unknown links, reminders and email drafts wait for your approval.

  • Circle to AskCtrl + Alt + C

    Drag around any screen area to read it once on your PC without saving the image. Separate Screen Awareness runs 5 to 30 minutes with a visible indicator.

Scratch notes and Wi-Fi transfer inside the panel

Write checklists, dictate directly into notes, or drop a file to share with your phone over local Wi-Fi without cables or cloud relays.

Notes with checklists and dictation

Mix plain text and inline checkboxes in the same note, search across notes instantly, or press Dictate to speak into your note locally.

Quick Transfer over your Wi-Fi

Drop files to generate a local QR code for your phone, with two-way send or receive, an optional 4-digit PIN and automatic link expiry.

Ten themes, OS cleanup and focus timers

Every colour in Krovi comes from theme tokens, paired with safe Recycle Bin cleanup and background focus timers (see how Krovi compares).

  • Themes10 built-in + Follow system

    Switch instantly across Graphite, Porcelain, Obsidian, Linen, Mochi, Arcade, Terracotta, Tide, Voxel and Pixel Meadow, or follow your Windows mode.

  • Clean My OSRecycle Bin only

    Scans temp folders, caches, crash dumps and old installers with real paths and sizes, skips files from the last 24 hours, and moves nothing before you confirm.

  • Clipboard, timers and PomodoroCounts in the pill

    Searchable clipboard history skips password-manager copies, while countdowns, a lap stopwatch and Pomodoro cycles keep ticking while closed.

Optional AI key, stored on your PC

Voice dictation, Agent Mode and every other local tool work without an account or API key. Add your own key only if you want Circle to Ask or Screen Awareness to query a provider (setup guide).

  1. Step 1 Create a key

    Generate an API key in the console for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq.

  2. Step 2 Save it in Settings

    Paste the key once under Settings and AI. Krovi stores it in Windows Credential Manager.

  3. Step 3 Connect directly

    Requests go straight from your PC to that provider when you press Ask, without a Krovi proxy.

  • Anthropic api.anthropic.com
  • OpenAI api.openai.com
  • Google Gemini generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  • Groq api.groq.com
