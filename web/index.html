Voice, planning and screen reading

Reopens the last tool you used, with the other eighteen on the left rail and live RAM, disk and CPU rings on Home.

  • Voice dictationCtrl + Alt + D

    On-device Whisper types at your cursor in any app (Tiny, Base or Small on AVX2 CPUs).

  • Agent ModeCtrl + Alt + A

    Local step planner; unfamiliar links, reminders and email drafts wait for approval.

  • Circle to AskCtrl + Alt + C

    Read any screen region locally once, or send that cropped region to your own AI key.

Scratch notes and local Wi-Fi transfer

Inline checklists with local dictation, plus QR file sharing over your own Wi-Fi.

Notes & dictation

Plain text, inline checkboxes, instant search and one-click Whisper dictation.

Quick Transfer

Send or receive files on your phone via local Wi-Fi QR, optional PIN and auto-expiry.

Themes, OS cleanup and focus

Instant token-based themes, safe Recycle Bin cleanup and focus timers that tick inside the closed pill.

  • Ten themesFollow system

    Graphite, Porcelain, Obsidian, Linen, Mochi, Arcade, Terracotta, Tide, Voxel and Pixel Meadow.

  • Clean My OSRecycle Bin

    Reviews temp folders, caches and crash dumps; skips recent 24h files and confirms before moving.

  • Clipboard & PomodoroLive in pill

    Searchable clipboard (ignoring password managers), multiple countdowns, stopwatch and Pomodoro.

Optional AI key

Local tools work without a key. Save your own key in Windows Credential Manager only for Circle to Ask or Screen Awareness (setup guide).

  1. Step 1 Create a key

    From Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Groq.

  2. Step 2 Save in Settings

    Stored locally in Windows Credential Manager.

  3. Step 3 Direct HTTPS

    Sent straight from your PC when you press Ask.

  • Anthropic api.anthropic.com
  • OpenAI api.openai.com
  • Google Gemini generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  • Groq api.groq.com
